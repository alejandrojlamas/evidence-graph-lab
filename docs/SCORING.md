# Anti-apophenia scoring

The Skeptic agent reviews every bridge entity proposed by the Analyst. Its job is to downgrade
easy associations and explain why a lead does—or does not—deserve human attention. It does not
prove a thesis.

## Input

The Skeptic receives a `BridgeCandidate` containing:

- the canonical node, type, and degree;
- a betweenness-based `bridge_score`;
- citable evidence for incident edges;
- operator-defined editorial labels in `source_side`.

## Checks

### Independence

Correlated evidence is collapsed by `source_side:source_name`. Five items from the same source
count as one unit. Convergence passes only when diversity is sufficient and at least two
`source_side` values are present.

This is an approximation: different outlets may still depend on the same news agency, statement,
or corporate group. The system does not reconstruct those dependencies on its own.

### Improbability

Observed betweenness is compared with random graphs generated by
`networkx.configuration_model`, preserving the degree distribution. The report includes the mean,
standard deviation, z-score, and p-value. A candidate passes when it satisfies both
`null_model_alpha` and `improbability_threshold`.

The result depends on corpus size and bias. It is not statistical proof of real-world behavior.

### Specificity

The score penalizes vague relationships: co-mentions alone, overly broad entities, and high-degree
nodes. It rewards non-trivial predicates and excerpts with roles, dates, numbers, and proper
names. The result is a minimal claim to review, not a conclusion.

### Temporal concentration

The method finds the window with the greatest evidence concentration and compares it with a
uniform baseline over the corpus timeline. Two editorial labels in the same window add a small
signal, but that signal alone cannot promote a lead.

## Statuses

- `promote_for_human_review`: passes the main thresholds and deserves priority review.
- `needs_more_evidence`: retains a signal but lacks a key supporting check.
- `degraded`: the bridge is weak or too expected.
- `discard`: independence or specificity is insufficient.

## Artifacts

- `data/output/bridge_candidates.json`: evidence-backed ranking.
- `data/output/skeptic_reports.json`: auditable breakdown of every check.

Scores order human research work. They do not certify facts, intent, causality, or responsibility.
